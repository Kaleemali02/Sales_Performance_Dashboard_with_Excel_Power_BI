# E-Commerce Sales Analysis — Excel & Power BI

**Author:** Kaleem Ali  
**Project type:** E-commerce business performance analysis  
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Pivot Tables, Charts, and forecasting

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce business performance using Excel and Power BI. It explores sales, profit, order volume, customer segments, shipping performance, product categories, and regional trends. The analysis is intended to support data-driven decisions and identify opportunities to improve business performance.

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and charts
- Forecasting
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## E-Commerce Business Performance Analysis

The Excel analysis and Power BI dashboard examine the following questions:

1. What are the historical trends in total sales and profit by year, and what are the forecasted sales and profit for the upcoming year?
2. What factors may explain the projected downward trend in sales and profit?
3. How does shipment time relate to sales and customer satisfaction across ship modes, and what could improve delivery performance while controlling costs?
4. How do average sales and profit compare across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments for each category?
5. Which product category could be considered for discounts, and why?
6. Which region recorded the highest order volume over the analyzed period?
7. What are the year-over-year changes in profit, sales, order count, and profit margin?
8. What actions could improve overall profit based on category and sub-category sales, profit, and margins?

## Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard presents key performance indicators, including:

- Total Profit
- Sales
- Total Quantity
- Number of Orders
- Profit Margin

It also visualizes monthly sales and profit trends, category-level sales and profit, sales by state, top customers, category sales share, and the top five sub-categories by sales. Filters are available for year, region, and segment.
